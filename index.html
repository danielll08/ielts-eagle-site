IELTS Eagle 🦅

IELTS Eagle is a personal desktop tool used by a single content creator to produce short educational videos about IELTS Writing and to upload them to the creator's own TikTok account.

Every teaching point in the videos is checked against public documents published on ielts.org. Illustrations and the eagle mascot are drawn by the creator with AI assistance and are labelled as AI-generated.

The tool uses TikTok Login Kit (to let the creator connect their own account) and the Content Posting API (to send finished videos to the creator's TikTok inbox, where the creator reviews and publishes them manually). It is not offered to other users.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/danielll08/ielts-eagle-site/issues.
